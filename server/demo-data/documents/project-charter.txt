PROJECT CHARTER: CLIENT PORTAL REFRESH
Acme Studio (fictional demo company)

Purpose
The client portal is where customers review drafts, approve deliverables and download final
files. The current version is four years old, slow on mobile and hard to maintain. This
project replaces it with a faster portal that keeps the familiar review flow.

Objectives
1. Page load under two seconds on a mid-range phone.
2. Review and approval in no more than three clicks from the project list.
3. Expiring, permission-based share links for every deliverable.
4. An audit trail of who viewed and downloaded each file.

Scope
In scope: project list, file review, comments, approvals, share links, notifications.
Out of scope: invoicing, time tracking and the internal resource planner.

Milestones
- Discovery and interviews: complete by 15 August.
- Design and prototype: complete by 12 September.
- Release 2.0 internal testing: from 15 September.
- Client pilot with three customers: October.
- General availability: November, subject to pilot feedback.

Roles
Sponsor: Priya (operations). Product and design: Marcus. Engineering: Lena and Ada.
Finance oversight: Tomas.

Risks
- Scope creep from pilot feedback. Mitigation: a single prioritised backlog, reviewed weekly.
- Data migration errors. Mitigation: dry runs on a copy, checksums compared after each run.
- Availability of pilot clients. Mitigation: five candidates approached, three needed.

Success measures
Pilot clients approve deliverables faster than on the old portal, support tickets about
downloads drop by half, and no deliverable is shared without an expiry date.
