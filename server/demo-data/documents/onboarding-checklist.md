# Onboarding checklist

Welcome to Acme Studio (a fictional company). Work through this list in your first two weeks
and tick items off with your buddy.

## Day one
- Collect your laptop and security key from operations.
- Sign in to the file workspace and set up your password manager.
- Read the employee handbook, especially the sections on data handling and travel.
- Join the team channels: general, engineering, design, finance.

## First week
- Meet your buddy for a walkthrough of current projects.
- Complete the security awareness module (about 40 minutes).
- Review the architecture overview and the release notes for the client portal.
- Shadow one client call and write three questions you had afterwards.
- Set up your development environment following the staging guide.

## Second week
- Pick up a small ticket from the sprint backlog and ship it with review.
- Present what you learned in the Friday demo slot (five minutes is plenty).
- Schedule a check-in with your manager to agree on goals for the first quarter.

## Good habits
- Keep working files in the shared workspace, not on your desktop.
- Share with clients using expiring links with the minimum permission needed.
- Report anything unusual, such as unexpected file changes or strange renames, to operations right away.
- Lock your screen when you step away, even in the office.
